About

FootageCredit searches open archives (Wikimedia Commons, NASA Image and Video Library, Internet Archive and Openverse) and shows the results side by side with plain-language license badges.

Clips that need attribution are added to your credits list when you download them. Copy the block into your video description and you're covered.

We don't host any files. Every download comes straight from the original archive, and the license shown is the one the archive reports. Licenses on archives can be wrong or incomplete, so check the source page before publishing anything important.
