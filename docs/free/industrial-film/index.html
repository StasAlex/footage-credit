    Search

    Type a topic, like “steam train” or “1950s kitchen”, and press Search. We check the Internet Archive, NASA, Wikimedia Commons and Openverse at once.

    Check the badge

    Green “commercial OK” means you can use it in a monetized video. “Credit required” means you must name the author. Orange means open the source page and check the license yourself.

    Click Download. If the clip needs a credit, it’s added to your Credit list (bottom right). Open it, click Copy and paste it into your video description.
